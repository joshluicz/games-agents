## Risks
- **Untyped:** no luau-lsp here, so `--!strict` type errors weren't checked; the Phase 4 probe used a strict
  property mock, which covers most of it.
- **Layout by numbers only:** card heights with AutomaticSize, chip widths (estimated ~10 px per letter), the More
  menu placement under safe insets, the wallet at 568.
- **Part budget:** the Building has 1 part of headroom at Lv 5 (454/455); rooms 2 (68/70).
- **Pace:** an idle clinic earns nothing without a Receptionist; one press per patient; a Lv 3+ card per patient;
  bays 5-6 up to ~90 studs from far seats.
- **HUD reveal:** a Lv 1 player who spent their cash and owns no nurse won't see the Nurses door until they can
  afford one again; old Lv 1 saves with no nurse or night cure see Shop and Book only at first (doc §7 #6).
- **Rail on short desktop windows:** the wallet grew to 160, so 440-533 px tall non-phone windows can overflow the
  rail's minimum 3 plates (foldables, small windows).
- **Invisicam vs creature eyes/glow:** written from knowledge of Roblox's PlayerModule, re-asserted per frame; check.
- **Remaining emoji in server toasts:** "🏠 No clinic yet!", "🔄 Tree reset!", "🔄 Tutorial restarted!" (🔄 is
  retired), the "🏢 AGENCY" kiosk sign.

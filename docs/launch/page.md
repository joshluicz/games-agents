# The Roblox page: Cure a Creature

Everything to paste into Creator Hub (Creations → the game → Configure). Images are in `images/`, made from real
Studio captures of the game (2026-11-16), as Roblox requires.

## Basic info
- **Name:** 🏥 Cure a Creature
- **Genre:** Simulation
- **Devices:** Computer, Phone, Tablet (Console off until controllers are tested)
- **Max players:** 6

## Description (paste as is)
```
🏥 Run your own clinic for the silliest creatures in town!

🧪 Make cures at your stations
🩹 Treat patients at the counter
🛏️ Watch them rest, then hop away happy!

⭐ Earn Stars to unlock new rooms: the Bath Room, Cozy Ward, Winter Wing and Sunny Garden
📖 Collect every creature in your Patient Book
🌙 Night shift: glowing night patients pay double!
👫 Play with friends: help at each other's clinics and send hearts
📬 Come back every day for mail, goals and visits from your favourite regulars

A cute pet hospital game: be the doctor, the vet and the boss of your own clinic.

Everyone welcome. Even the weird ones. 💖
```
Search words it carries: hospital, pet, vet, doctor, clinic, creatures, cute. No links (Roblox rule for this audience).

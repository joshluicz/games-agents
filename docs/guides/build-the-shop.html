Build the Shop

You're building the place where the whole game happens. You don't need building experience: Roblox Studio has an AI helper called Assistant, and you tell it what to make. Joshua's code runs the customers, cash and upgrades. Your job is to make the shop look amazing.

Roblox Studio on your MacPart A: start nowPart B: when Joshua says Team Create is ready

Part A: build it in your own place

Practice place. Nothing you do here can break the real game.

  1. Open a blank place

    Open Roblox Studio, then New and Baseplate.

  2. Open Explorer and Properties

    Explorer lists everything in your game. Properties shows the settings of whatever you click. Both are in the View (or Window) menu.

  3. Open Assistant

    Click Assistant in Studio. A chat panel opens: type what you want and it builds it.

  4. Build the shop

    Copy this into Assistant:

    Paste into Assistant

    Create a Model in Workspace named Shop. Inside it, build a small, cozy shop: a flat 40 by 40 stud wooden floor, walls about 12 studs tall on three sides with the front side left open as the entrance, and a counter 14 studs wide near the back wall, named Counter. Leave space behind the counter for players to stand. Anchor every part. Keep the floor flat and empty from the open front all the way to the counter.

  5. Add the 5 special parts

    The code looks for these exact names, so spell them exactly like this, capital letters too. Copy this into Assistant:

    Paste into Assistant

    Inside the Shop model, add these anchored parts with exactly these names. Station: a 4 by 5 by 3 stud block behind the counter, where players will make food. QueueStart: an invisible 2 by 1 by 2 stud part on the floor, 3 studs in front of the counter, on the customer side. CustomerSpawn: an invisible 2 by 1 by 2 stud part on the floor just inside the open front. CustomerExit: an invisible 2 by 1 by 2 stud part on the floor just inside the open front, 8 studs to the side of CustomerSpawn. Invisible means Transparency 1 and CanCollide false. Keep the Counter part named Counter.

  6. Check the names

    In Explorer, click the arrow next to Shop. Tick each one you can see (extra parts are fine):

    Missing or spelled wrong? Click it and press F2 to rename it.

  7. Make it look good (the fun part)

    Ask Assistant for anything you like. Some ideas:

    Ideas

    Make the walls pastel pink and the floor light wood. Add a sign above the entrance that says OPEN, some shelves on the side walls, and warm lights hanging from the ceiling.

    For a cool machine or oven: click Station in Explorer first, then type /generate_mesh a cute cartoon oven. Assistant makes it fit inside Station's box. Then click the old Station part and set Transparency to 1.

    Don't delete or rename Station.The code needs it to know where food gets made.

  8. Walk around it

    Press Play (or F5) to walk around, and Stop when you're done. No customers will show up here. That's normal, because the code lives in the real game.

  9. Save it

    File → Save to File keeps a copy on your Mac. Then in Explorer, right-click Shop → Save to Roblox. That puts it in your inventory for Part B.

Part B: put it in the real game

Do this once Joshua says Team Create is ready.
First:you and Joshua are Roblox friends, then Trusted Friends, and he's given you Edit access. At 13, Roblox needs this (or a parent's OK) before you can build with someone 18+.

  1. Open the real game

    In Studio, find it under games shared with you and open it. That's Team Create: Joshua sees what you build, live.

  2. Drop your shop in

    Open the Toolbox, go to Inventory → My Models, and click your Shop.

  3. Check where it sits

    In Explorer, Shop must sit directly inside Workspace, not inside another folder. If there's another model called Shop, tell Joshua. There can only be one.

  4. Press Play

    Customers should walk in from the front and line up at your counter. 🎉

Rules

Don't touch the scripts.Leave ServerScriptService, StarterPlayerScripts and ReplicatedStorage → Shared alone. That's Joshua's code, and changes made there get wiped.
Free models can hide bad scripts.If something made by someone else gets added, click its arrow in Explorer. If there's a Script inside, delete it, or ask Joshua.
Make it your own.Don't copy another game's look, characters or name.
Keep the path clear.The floor from the entrance to the counter stays flat and empty. Customers walk in straight lines.

Stuck?

1. Ask Assistant: "How do I move a part?", "How do I make this bigger?", "Why can't I see my part?"

2. Still stuck? Tell Joshua what you tried and what happened.

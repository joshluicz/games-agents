# The game plan (v1, 2026-11-16)

**Ruled by Joshua today:** a co-op shop sim with a night twist · built for **under-13 players** · goal **make real
money** · Joshua (18+, Windows, Claude Code) and his brother (13, Mac). Evidence: `docs/research/2026-11-16-market-scan.md`.
Accounts and costs: `docs/setup/02-accounts-and-costs.md`.

## 1. The game in one line
> Run a little shop with your friends: serve customers, earn cash, upgrade. Then the sun goes down and the
> *night customers* come in.

- **Solo-fun, better with friends.** A new game has almost no players, so it can't need a full server.
- **A reason to come back tomorrow:** upgrades and shop unlocks that persist, plus a daily reward.
- **A reason to bring a friend:** more hands serve faster, and later a "friends in server" cash bonus. This feeds
  Roblox's co-play signal directly.
- **The night twist is spooky-cute, never scary.** Content has to rate **Minimal or Mild** to reach Kids (5–8).
- ⬜ **Theme and name: the brothers pick** (OPEN #1). The code calls the product a "Treat" until then. Theme ideas,
  none ruled: bakery · ice cream · ramen · pet grooming · toy repair.

## 2. Tonight's version (definition of done for 2026-11-16)
1. Customers walk in, queue at the counter and show an order with a patience bar.
2. Hold to make the product at the station, carry it, serve the customer, get cash.
3. Three upgrades that save: **Faster Hands** (make time) · **Bigger Tray** (carry more) · **Tip Jar** (more cash).
4. Day and night: night customers glow and pay double.
5. Progress saves when you rejoin.
6. The brother's first pass at the shop is built around the marker parts (§4).
7. Both brothers have playtested it in a **private** place, on PC and on a phone-sized screen (Studio's device emulator).

## 4. The contract between code and world (for the brother's build)
The code looks for a model called **`Shop`** in Workspace with these parts inside it. If they're missing, it builds a
grey placeholder shop so Joshua can test right away. The brother replaces the placeholder with a real build, keeping
these **names**:

| Part name | What it is | How to place it |
|---|---|---|
| `QueueStart` | Where the first customer stands | Just in front of the counter (about 3 studs). The queue grows backwards, away from the counter. Which way the part faces doesn't matter |
| `CustomerSpawn` | Where customers appear | By the door, inside the walls |
| `CustomerExit` | Where customers walk to leave | By the door (it can sit next to `CustomerSpawn`) |
| `Station` | The machine or oven you make the product at | Behind the counter, reachable by players |
| `Counter` | The counter itself | Anything goes. It's decoration plus a solid barrier |

Marker parts can be invisible (Transparency 1, CanCollide off, Anchored). Keep the floor between the door and
`QueueStart` clear and flat, because customers walk in straight lines.

## 6. Money rules for a kids' game (red line 3)
Passes sell **convenience or looks**: 2× Cash, a helper, shop skins. No paid random items at launch. No countdown
pressure, no "buy now or lose it". Nothing that lets a payer beat another player. Every purchase gets validated on
the server.

## 7. Risks, said once
- **It gets no players.** That's the normal outcome for a first game. Stage 2 is the test, and the 60-day window is
  the deadline.
- **Hours.** This competes with university applications, Eitri Brokkr and the bird frame (the brother's holiday
  month). `../joshua-hq` decides the hours; this plan doesn't.
- **Money flows to Joshua's account or group.** How the brother gets his share has to be in writing first (OPEN #2).

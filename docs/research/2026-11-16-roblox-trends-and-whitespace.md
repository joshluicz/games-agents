## Breakout mechanics, and which fit a kids' game
- **Fits:** a plot friends can visit and admire; free weather or visitor events that change play; rare customers you
  *see*, never buy; weekly live parties; capped offline progress; making things together.
- **Avoid:** stealing (and paid protection), paid restocks or skips, open trading (real-money sales and scams), paid
  random eggs, sell-out timers.
- **What lasts:** Bee Swarm's multi-year run is credited to avoiding aggressive monetisation (RoWatcher). The games that
  endure (Adopt Me!, Brookhaven, Bee Swarm) win on a steady update rhythm. A two-person team can't out-race clone
  studios, but it can out-last them.

## Live-ops that bring kids back
- A fixed weekly update day: Steal An Egg on Saturdays, Brookhaven on Fridays, Adopt Me! updating on Friday plus a live
  Saturday session.
- 99 Nights runs a weekly "Update Party".
- Seasonal events. Badges from Roblox's own events (one Hollow badge was awarded 1.08M times in a day†).
- **Flagged:** loseable streaks (EU KIDS Act proposal 2026-09-17, Digital Fairness Act proposal 2026-11-11, neither law
  yet); false scarcity; paid random items; pay-to-progress; weekly exclusives you can only claim while online (make
  them claimable later).

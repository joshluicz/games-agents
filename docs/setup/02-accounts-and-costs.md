## 🚨 The gate that matters: getting to under-13 players
A new game is **first shown only to age-checked players 16+**. It becomes eligible for Kids and Select accounts once it
records **250 unique plays by "highly engaged" age-checked players within 60 days**. Roblox counts a player as highly
engaged based on account age, playtime in *your* game, and having **bought something anywhere on Roblox in the last 60
days**. It's checked hourly, and you can track it on the **Audience Reach** dashboard in Creator Hub.
The refundable fee comes back **90 days after the game becomes eligible**, or 90 days after paying if it never does. The
exception is a game permanently removed for breaking Community Standards.
▶️ **So the game has to be fun for 16+ players too**, at least enough to earn those 250 plays, even though kids are the
real audience. `docs/plan/` treats this as launch problem #1.

## How the money works
- **Creator share:** 70% of the Robux spent on your game passes and developer products. Roblox keeps 30%.
- **Creator Rewards** replaced Premium Payouts on 2025-07-24. It pays a little per engaged session by an "Active Spender",
  plus 35% of a new player's first $100 spent (ID-verified owner).
- **DevEx (Robux → cash):** you must be **13+**, hold **30,000 earned Robux** minimum, have a verified email and a tax
  form on file (W-8 if you're not a US person). Rate: **US$0.0038 per Robux**, so 30,000 Robux = **US$114**. One
  cash-out per calendar month.
- **The sobering maths:** a player spends 100 Robux on a pass → you earn 70 → that's about US$0.27 at DevEx. Your first
  US$114 cash-out needs roughly **43,000 Robux spent by players**. Roblox's own figures: the *median* DevEx recipient
  earned **US$1,550** over 12 months, and that counts only the ~35,000 creators who already clear the minimum.

## Ownership (red line 5: in writing before money)
- **Group-owned** is the clean route. Joshua owns the group. The brother's role has Edit (and Publish if wanted).
  Recurring payouts can split a game's Robux by percentage between members.
- ⬜ **Unconfirmed:** whether a 13-year-old can receive group payouts or DevEx without a parent's consent. Roblox's pages
  mention "eligibility checks" only. Settle this in the ownership note before the first Robux.

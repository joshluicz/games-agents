# Nurses come on a shared conveyor belt in town, at set times

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim:**
> ANOTHER way to do the hiring of nurses / doctors is to use a conveyor belt like steal a brainrot...

> then the nurses are on a conveyor belt and you can buy them, and they come at certain times...

Then, from Claude's options: **"One shared belt in town (Recommended)"**.

## What this rules
1. **No capsule roll.** Nurses ride a **shared conveyor belt in the town plaza**, and anyone can buy the one they see
   with cash. The belt is the mechanic, borrowed as a genre format (red line 2). There's no stealing, and no names,
   art or characters from that game.
2. **Set times:** a nurse steps on every few seconds, and a **Night Rush** every in-game night raises the Rare+ odds.
   It runs on the game's own day/night clock, not real-world timers.
3. **Kid-fair (Claude's design):**
   - the odds are on the sign;
   - a server-wide pity (a Rare+ at least every 8 arrivals);
   - a short per-player hire cooldown;
   - Commons are plentiful and priced by the viewer's hospital level;
   - no Robux anywhere.

   Numbers in `docs/plan/2026-11-16-depth-update-design.md` §2.

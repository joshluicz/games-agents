# The creatures go blocky

**Joshua, 2026-11-17 (chat), verbatim:**
> blocky.

This was his reply to Claude's question: round, blocky, or blocky with changes. He saw the side-by-side shot
`logs/playtests/2026-11-17-look-spike/4-creature-side-by-side.jpg`. Claude had recommended keeping them round, as
close but not a clear win and a big job.

## What it decides
1. **Every creature, patients and nurses, is rebuilt from blocks.**
   - Studs go on the big upward faces.
   - Mid-value colours, not pastel.
   - Flat block faces: big eyes with glints, blush, a small mouth.
2. **The rig contract stays,** so Patients, Nurses, the acting props and the client motion don't change: `Root`,
   `Core`, `PoseJoint`, the sockets, `IconAnchor` and the moods.

## Lessons from the prototype (Claude)
- **Square ears read cat-like.** Give each species its own ear shape: rounded stacks, flops, or tufts.
- **Small 1-stud parts show cut-off studs.** Keep studs on the big blocks only.
- **The look must stay our own.** Red line 2 rules out copying another game's pets.
  - Each species keeps its own silhouette, colours and "Bits" (a bell, glasses, a cherry) from its `Look`.

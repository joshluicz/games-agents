# The look overhaul: Claude's default calls

**Source:**
- The decisions in §7 of `docs/research/2026-11-16-hospital-building.md`.
- The open points of `docs/plan/2026-11-17-look-overhaul-build-plan.md`.

Claude told Joshua these calls on 2026-11-17 and invited him to overrule any of them. Each one is a single edit to
Config or a builder.

1. **The building starts at Lv 1.** The tent goes. The Stand becomes a small clinic with the Counter as its reception
   desk, and it keeps the name "First-Aid Stand". Names stay the brothers' call.
2. **One bold plot colour on the whole building.** It's studded, with warm trim, a dark roof and glass. The alternative,
   a white hospital with coloured bands, was not taken.
3. **Solid walls upstairs,** and the floors above you turn see-through on your own screen only.
4. **Wards look full** (`-002`):
   - 7 beds on the ward floor: 3 usable plus 4 just for show, 2 of them with sleeping stand-ins.
   - 2 beds at Lv 1.
   - The usable bed count is unchanged; raising it is the plan's §F1.
5. **A placeholder paw-heart logo** replaces every cross. The brothers design the real one.
6. **Lean scenery,** to keep 400 parts a plot at Lv 5: wider fence posts, one-cube bushes, tents become annexes.
7. **A tutorial Skip button.** The floor beam stays on until 3 cures, and Replay sits in ⚙️ (`-002`).
8. **The guide nurse** has a placeholder name and look (the cast is OPEN #1).
9. **Creatures stay round** until Joshua judges the blocky prototype (`logs/playtests/2026-11-17-look-spike/4-*.jpg`).

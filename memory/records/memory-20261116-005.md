# Creature Clinic, evergreen, launching with a Winter Wing

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim**, after three theme options (Night Inn, Creature Clinic, Glow Aquarium):
> the thing is that halloween has passed...
> christmas is next... making it centred for either is good and bad. good cos we can ride the season, bad cos of
> continuity. id lean towards the season cos i just wanna make a quick and good game...
> whas ur call.
> i like the hospital one, personally, a little more than cafes cos those are quite oversaturated...
> your call...

## What this rules (Claude's call)
1. **Theme: a creature clinic** on the own-plot structure (`memory-20261116-003`). Players cure an original absurd cast
   of creatures (`-004`). The design is `docs/plan/2026-11-16-creature-clinic-design.md`.
2. **Season as a layer, not the core.** The game launches with a **Winter Wing** (winter patients, snowy decor, a
   winter event) aimed at the school holidays. The wing stays, and the event returns each December.

## Why
- **Joshua prefers the clinic.**
- **Demand is proven:** a horror animal hospital (Animal Hospital, Anomaly) peaked at 1.3M players (RoWatcher†).
- **The cozy, owned, decorated version is open,** and caring for creatures is an honest loop for kids.
- **The restaurant lane is crowded and cold:** ~28K players combined across the top 7 food tycoons on 2026-11-07
  (rbxtcg).
- **Seasonal events measurably win players:** games with an October event grew, and those without one shrank
  (Studiokrew, Oct 2026).
- **A Christmas-only game dies on 26 Dec.** A wing keeps the work.
- `docs/research/2026-11-16-roblox-trends-and-whitespace.md`.

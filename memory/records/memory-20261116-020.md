# Depth update tuning: level spawns, no Golden Hour countdown, GOOD! holds the combo

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim:**
> your call for all.

Asked about build plan §E5–E7 of `docs/plan/2026-11-16-depth-update-build-plan.md` (OPEN-QUESTIONS #8).

## Claude's calls
1. **Levels pay (§E5):**
   - Patients come every 13/11/10/9/8 s at hospital levels 1–5.
   - `Config.MaxQueue = 3` still caps the line, so faster spawns only reward players who keep up and never
     pile up on a slow one.
   - Each recipe item after the first pays +50%.
   - Belt prices scale ×1.0 to ×1.4 by level.
2. **Golden Hour (§E6):** no countdown anywhere, ever. A ticking "only 2 minutes left!" clock on a chance at a rare
   nurse is the FOMO pressure red line 3 rules out. One 6 s banner with the true odds instead.
3. **Combo (§E7):**
   - A GOOD! holds the 🔥 combo (it doesn't grow and doesn't reset).
   - A PERFECT! adds +1.
   - Only a miss resets it to "Go!".
   - Young players shouldn't see a good result read as losing.

Tunable after playtests.

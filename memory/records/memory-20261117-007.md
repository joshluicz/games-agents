# A calm UI with emojis used correctly

**Joshua, 2026-11-17 (chat), verbatim:**
> the UI is cluttered. can you use taste-skill and impeccable in studio-agents to fix all the UI. see if there are
> skills online for roblox UI...
>
> the UI looks very AI with all the emojis...
>
> not to remove the emojis, but to use them correctly...

The direction: `docs/plan/2026-11-17-ui-direction.md` (taste-skill, impeccable, the repo's roblox-ui and game-ui-ux
skills; its rule R0 is `memory-20261117-006`'s "simple, especially at the start").

**Claude asked four questions. Joshua picked the recommended option on each:**
1. **Team card:** one main button (the nurse's job); **Sell and List go behind "More"** (shown once you own 2 nurses).
2. **Panel titles in words only** ("Team", "Shop"). Emojis stay where they mean something: items, cases, tools,
   money next to its number, and the HUD buttons.
3. **The HUD reveals one door at a time**, each when it first has content (Nurses when the first nurse can be hired,
   Mail with the first letter, Golden Hour at Lv 3, Market after the Agency has been used). Players who already
   have a door keep it.
4. **Progress bars in navy**, so green only means "go here / do this".

The rest of the direction (emoji roles and sizes, quieter buttons and cards, the change list) is Claude's call within
these answers; the open items in the doc's §7 not asked here (emoji collision winners, the Agency's icon, older saves
keeping their doors) are Claude's proposals as written there.

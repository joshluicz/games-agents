# Boosts, and tiered achievement badges (Bee Swarm style)

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim:**
> and there should be boosts associated with these.
> add a whole badge mechanic too.... like bee swarm simulator.

## What this rules (Claude's design)
1. **Boosts are earned only:**
   - **Permanent** boosts come from Doctor ranks (+2% to +15% Cash) and from badge tiers.
   - **Caps:** Cash +50%, make speed +40%, patience +50%, tips +50%.
   - **Timed** boosts come from daily play gifts (Quick Hands, Lucky Tips, Star Rush). They tick only while playing,
     extend rather than stack, and cap at 60 min.
2. **Tiered badges** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Ruby, Mythic) live in a 🏅 Badge Book:
   - Healer (total cures), one per wing, one per station, Night Shift, Visitor, Storyteller, Friendly, Builder and
     Dedication (play time).
   - It's list-driven, so later badges (Perfect mini-games, nurses, hospital level) are easy to add.
   - Each tier pays cash + Stars and a small permanent boost.
   - Roblox badges only for Healer Gold and the Doctor ranks.
3. **Never Robux** (red line 3). The paid 2× Cash pass (`-006`) stays separate and cash-only.

## What this rules
1. **Treating = short mini-games + recipes.** Each treatment is a 3–5 s mini-game (pour, patch, scrub, cool) rated
   OK/Good/Perfect; never a fail (kids). Perfect gives a small cash tip and builds a combo. Later patients need a
   2–3 item recipe.
2. **Pet nurses by rolling, for in-game cash only.** Odds are printed on the capsule, duplicates level a nurse up,
   and there's a pity guarantee. **No Robux rolls** (red line 3: paid random items for kids are banned for minors in
   some countries and need odds on Roblox; it also pressures kids to spend). Nurses only work while their owner is
   in the game (no offline/AFK income, `memory-20261116-003`).
3. **The UI gets juice:** count-ups, flying coins, celebration screens, capsule-opening animation, sounds and bouncy
   buttons. Kit's reduced-motion and calm toggles still apply.
4. **A performance pass,** measured first.

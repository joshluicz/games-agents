## What this rules (design §8 of `docs/plan/2026-11-16-depth-update-design.md`)
1. **The roster:** 3 nurses, +1 per rebirth. Every owned nurse works; the Journal keeps history.
2. **Each rebirth gives:** a perk card (choose 1 of 3), tree points, +25% cash/Stars, +1 roster slot, content unlocks
   (R1 Mythic/Secret + a wing, R2 A&E helicopter + Pharmacy 2, R3 golden patients + nurse cap +5, R4 Night Clinic,
   R5 a rebirth-only Legendary + mascot), a branch theme, and status (a Branches badge, a Most Rebirths board).

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim:**
> my brother had good ideas. first he questioned what the nurses would do...
> i said that they treated patients and earned more per patient. but i think we can increase the depth. and it comes
> hand in hand with the complxity...
> the next thing is that he said that the nurses should spawn less often than in steal a brainrot.... cos in SAB its
> so frequent that it loses its rarity. you need to feel like you earned it...
> and i want the progression to be amazing. add rebirths too... but add abilities and depth to each rebirth...

## What this rules (design: `docs/plan/2026-11-16-depth-update-design.md` §7)
1. **Nurses:**
   - jobs (Maker, Healer, Porter, Receptionist, Cheerer, Medic)
   - a department specialty
   - one signature ability that scales with rarity
   - XP to level 10, with ability upgrades
   - team bonuses
2. **A rarer belt:** about one nurse per 40 s; base odds Common 75 · Rare 20 · Epic 4.5 · Legendary 0.5; Mythic/Secret
   only in events; Epic+ gated to hospital level 3+ and Mythic/Secret to the first rebirth; a longer pity. The
   numbers are tunable after playtests.
3. **Rebirths ("open a new branch"):**
   - Optional.
   - Resets cash, upgrades, level and rooms. Keeps every collection, nurses, badges, rank, decor and Stars.
   - Each rebirth gives Branch Ability Tree points (Care / Speed / Fortune) and +25% cash/Stars.
   - Each rebirth unlocks new content and a new cosmetic branch theme.
4. **Kids' rules hold:** no Robux, a clear keep/reset screen, and no pressure to rebirth (red line 3).

# Simple start: you send the patients, fewer places at first

**Joshua, 2026-11-17 (chat, after playing), verbatim:**
> when the patient first arrives, he should sit down at the chair.
>
> the door is so low, things look cluttered... why is the hot chocolate taken from that room. how did they even get
> themselves there. perhaps we can add a receptionist mechanic?? cos having them randomly spawn there is funny...
>
> the purple bits are glitching. its very cramped especially for someone claustrophobic.

Claude asked three questions. **Joshua's answers, verbatim:**
- Reception: "you do it. but no need to add receptionist desk perhaps. perhaps its just sending them to the correct
  ward or place for treatment. and we can cut down the number of places / treatment for the initial start. the game
  is getting a little too complicated already. need it simple especially at the start."
- Hot chocolate: "minimise the patients walking around. yes move the hot cocoa into like a pantry or something..."
- Space: "Bigger building + camera fix (Recommended)" (Claude's option: the ground floor about 1.5x bigger, wider
  corridors and roomier bays and rooms, low dividers between bays, walls go see-through between you and the camera).

## What it decides
1. **Simple at the start.** Fewer places and treatments at Lv 1; more unlock with levels. Simplicity beats depth
   when they clash.
2. **The player routes patients.** Patients come in and sit in the waiting room; the player sends each one to the
   right place for its treatment. No receptionist desk mechanic.
3. **Patients walk as little as possible.** Supplies come to the patient, not the patient to the supply. Hot
   chocolate moves to a pantry.
4. **Bigger, open building** with a see-through-walls camera.
5. The details (which cases at Lv 1, how routing looks, the pantry's place, the new sizes) are Claude's proposals,
   shown to Joshua before the build; he didn't rule on them here.

# Progression v2: the open points take the plan's defaults

**Source:** §E of `docs/plan/2026-11-16-progression-v2-build-plan.md` (branch `claude/progression-v2`). There are 20
points there, each with its reason. Joshua delegated the last round of calls ("your call for all", `-020`). These
are the same kind of tuning calls, so Claude took the defaults so the build could start. Every number is in
Config, so overruling one costs a single edit.

## The calls that touch a brothers' ruling (flag these to Joshua)
1. **The roster stops at 8.** That is 3, +1 per rebirth, to R5. memory-019 set no top. Every owned nurse works on
   the plot at up to 40 parts each, so 8 is the perf ceiling. Later rebirths still give everything else.
2. **Nurses above the roster rest (💤).** This covers saves from Update 1 that own more than 3. They are never deleted.
3. **No clocks.** The Agency says "New nurses every 5 min" with no ticking timer. The design's belt track and
   Agency countdown are ruled out by red line 3 and `-020`.
4. **Fortune never changes a roll.** Every player sees the same true odds. Bargain makes nurses cheaper instead,
   and Lucky Stock gives extra Agency picks at the shown odds.

## The rest (Claude's design calls)
- **Abilities:** they fire by themselves.
- **Night Owl:** night patients keep coming at your clinic for 30% of a night.
- **Twin Counters:** the counter serves the 1st or 2nd patient in line.
- **Escort:** a bonus (+20%), never required.
- **Triage:** from Lv 3, +10% for a right pick, automatic after 4 s.
- **Ambulance bay:** moves to Lv 2, following §6.
- **Lv 5:** builds the helipad; the air ambulance flies from R2.
- **Rebirth bonus:** +25% per rebirth, added not multiplied, counted to R20.
- **Market:** refuses a nurse you already have.
- **Sell-back:** 30% of base price per copy.
- **R5 Legendary:** can't be sold.
- **Ability tree:** resets for free.
- **Spent perk cards:** become +2 tree points.
- **R6–R10:** each gives a trophy decor.
- **Mascot:** stays by the Counter.
- **R1 wing:** a placeholder 🌻 Sunny wing (names: OPEN #1).

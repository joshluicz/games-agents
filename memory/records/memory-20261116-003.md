# Your own place on a plot, kids back daily because it's fun, and not another restaurant tycoon

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat), verbatim:**
> So I think we need to improve the game idea and all. Perhaps make it more of a simulator idea. [...] it can be like a
> more like a restaurant tycoon, that kind of simulator-ish kind of games. [...] the thinking behind it and the depth
> is very low. Perhaps add more of a little bit of a storyline and idea behind of it, and give it expansion ideas.

Asked to finish *"The most important part is to keep the children…"*:
> keep the children coming back every day because it's fun

Offered three structures (A: one shared shop, today's build · **B: your own restaurant on a plot** · C: solo world),
Claude recommending B:
> lets do B.

Minutes later, while trend research ran:
> wait but my restaurant and restaurant tycoon 3 already exists.. i dont want another competitor.

## What this rules
1. **Structure: each player owns their own place on a plot in a shared server.** It starts small and grows; the
   player decorates it. Friends can join to help or visit. This builds on `memory-20261116-002` (co-op shop sim,
   under-13s, money): the co-op becomes "help in a friend's place".
2. **The design goal: kids come back every day because it's fun.** Missing a day must never punish; red line 3 still
   rules out spend pressure.
3. **Not a plain restaurant tycoon.** The theme or twist has to set it apart from the established restaurant tycoons.

## Prior art: why the established tycoons lose kids (Joshua, who has played both with his brother)
> yes we've played both. they get boring after a few days of playing and when the progression becomes stale becaus eyou
> have afk for long to get the next upgrade etc. and many kids cant afk cos theyre using their parent's ipad whatsoever...

So the design must give real progress from **playing**, not waiting: no upgrade gated behind long AFK, and a short
session on a borrowed iPad must still move things forward.

## Not ruled
The theme or twist (OPEN #1, now also "what makes it different") · the storyline · the expansion roadmap · the full
design (being written from trend and audience research, 2026-11-16) · the brother's view (Joshua was asked to check).

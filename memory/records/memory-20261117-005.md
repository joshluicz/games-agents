## What it decides
1. **Realistic cases, gentle.** A few real cases (a cough, a broken arm...). No needles, no blood, no surgery.
2. **Each visit: examine -> treat -> dispense.**
   - The exam is one short mini-game with a real tool (a stethoscope, a thermometer, an X-ray) that reveals the case.
   - The first cases show the answer.
   - A treatment step (a cast, a bandage) is a mini-game.
   - Medicine is prepared and handed over by the player at a **dispensary**. Nurses dispensing comes later.
3. **No "cures" in the game's words: treatments.** Keeping the title "Cure a Creature" or renaming it is a separate
   call for both brothers (`-009`; red line 4). Claude keeps the title until they decide (OPEN #12).
4. **The clinic is big and realistic from Lv 1.** Levels open rooms and floors instead of growing a small podium.
   This supersedes the growth part of `-008`.
5. **No wood floors** (done, `d54591a`). **The display window becomes a door** (in the redesign).
6. **Claude's call for everything else** (the cases, the mini-games, the layout, the numbers, the save migration).

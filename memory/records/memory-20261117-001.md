# The look overhaul: studs, a real hospital, side rails, calmer UI

**Joshua, 2026-11-16 (chat, after publishing Update 1), verbatim:**
> published. pretty shit. conveyor should be in the centre... then, the colours still feel wrong. too bright... can you
> see what research and the best games use then copy. ux feels segmented and not cohesive. tutorial is meh. needs
> arrows, UI can be more entertaining. the hospital looks dull, boring. only one hospital bed... its very very dull. i
> dont want a pop up tent. i want an actual hospital... they lack texture. is the builder for multiple levels, ie not a
> single story building. it should be a building. there should be some templates existing, we can take reference but it
> should be out own one...

> buttons are all at the button so the top feels bare. the layout doesnt seem methodical. feels empty... find the best
> practices and best uis.

> theres a trending texture for most of the steal a... games. its like a lego brick floor and the items / pets are all
> like lego.

> the arrow for tutorial should be at the bottom. ie closer to the floor than an overhead. im not too sure for this one
> but most games i know connect the middle of the player body to the arrow, to the objective.

**His picks (2026-11-17, from Claude's question):**
- Side rails (Recommended).
- Dark navy header (Recommended).
- Yes, calmer (Recommended).
- Prototype one blocky (Recommended).

## What it decides
1. **The world takes the studs look.**
   - Studs go on lawns, plot floors, walls, trees and prop tops.
   - The road stays smooth and dark.
   - There are dark anchors (road, plot pads, roofs) and no pastel lerp-to-white.
   - The lighting is relit.
   - Spec: `docs/research/2026-11-16-art-direction.md`.
   - Studio spike 2026-11-17, not saved: `logs/playtests/2026-11-17-look-spike/`.
2. **The hospital is a real building.**
   - It has storeys, textured walls, windows, an entrance, rows of beds and real rooms.
   - It's our own design: references are taken as ideas only (red line 2).
   - Design: `docs/research/2026-11-16-hospital-building.md`.
3. **The Nurse Belt runs down the centre of the main road.** The spawn plaza moves off the centre line to make room.
4. **The HUD sits on two side rails, with a top-centre "Now" card.**
   - Left rail, menus: Shop, Nurses, Book, Badges, Top.
   - Right rail, rewards waiting: Gifts, Goals, Mail.
   - Top centre: one "Now" card (tutorial or tracked goal).
   - Top right: the clock, the next free event (`-022`) and the visitor.
   - On phones: 3 a side, with Badges and Top under ⚙️.
   - Map: `docs/research/2026-11-16-ux-cohesion-audit.md` §3.1.
5. **One panel header colour: dark navy**, the same as the Now card.
6. **Calmer UI.**
   - One fixed chrome, and the HUD rails share one fill.
   - Candy colour only for rewards, rarity and the one main button.
   - Gloss, outlines and pop stay.
   - This replaces the candy-pop specifics of `-007`. "Saturated, flashy, trending" stands.
7. **The tutorial is guided by a floor-level beam from the player's body to the objective.**
   - The step card sits top centre ("TUTORIAL n/N").
   - The target glows.
   - Joshua was unsure about the beam ("im not too sure for this one"), so check its feel in a playtest.
   - Spec: `docs/research/2026-11-16-tutorial-ux.md`.
8. **Creatures stay round for now.** Claude builds one blocky, studded prototype (Hum Hamster) beside the round one for
   Joshua to judge, before converting any.

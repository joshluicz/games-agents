## What this rules
1. **The customers are an ORIGINAL cast of absurd, funny characters** in the brainrot spirit (silly hybrids, catchy
   names and sounds that kids collect and quote). **No meme characters, ever** (red line 2). Clean humour only: no
   bombs, weapons or crude jokes, so the rating stays Minimal or Mild for Roblox Kids.
2. **Title (Claude's call, delegated):** the working title does **not** lean on "brainrot". Re-check at the Stage 2
   go-public gate. If the word still pulls then, a brainrot title can be tested, since renaming takes a minute.

## Why (research of 2026-11-16, `docs/research/2026-11-16-roblox-trends-and-whitespace.md` and
## `docs/research/2026-11-16-kid-culture-and-roblox-features.md`)
- **The trend is fading and crowded:**
  - Steal a Brainrot measured 276K (2026-10-29) and 81K (2026-11-07), though trackers disagree.
  - There are 105 "Steal a ___" clones, and the original holds ~92% of their players.
  - Novelty waves have about a one-month half-life.
  - Our launch is weeks away.
- **IP risk is live:** Mementum Labs claims rights in "Tung Tung Tung Sahur" and is in a lawsuit with a company behind
  Steal a Brainrot (GamesBeat, 2026-08-06).
- **What kids are into right now** (absurd comedy, food with faces, squishy and collectible things, creatures,
  spooky-cute) fits an original absurd cast well.
- **Discovery is driven by retention and co-play**, not the title keyword (create.roblox.com/docs/discovery).
